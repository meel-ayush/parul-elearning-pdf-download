⚡ Download Parul PDF (Apple Shortcut)
Official iCloud Link

Tap the blue button below on your iPhone or iPad. iOS will open Apple's Shortcuts app where you can add it to your system in one tap.

⚡ Install "Download Parul PDF" Shortcut
1
Install the Shortcut on iOS
Tap the Install Shortcut button above. When prompted by iOS Shortcuts, tap Add Shortcut.
2
Enable "Allow Running Scripts" in iOS Settings
Open your device's Settings > Shortcuts > Advanced, then toggle ON "Allow Running Scripts".
⚙️
This official iOS permission allows Apple Shortcuts to safely inspect the active Safari lecture tab and extract the document link.
3
Open Your Lecture & Tap Share
In Safari, open your course document on elearning.paruluniversity.ac.in. Tap the Safari Share button ([↑]) at the bottom of the screen, scroll down, and tap Download Parul PDF.
4
First Permission Prompt — Tap "Allow"
The very first time you trigger the shortcut on your classroom, iOS displays a security prompt: "Allow Download Parul PDF to access elearning.paruluniversity.ac.in?" Tap Allow.
🔐
This gives the shortcut permission to inspect the active tab's lecture document URL. It only asks this once.
5
Second Permission Prompt — Tap "Allow Always"
Next, the shortcut will prompt to open the extracted document link. Tap Allow Always (or Allow) so your iPhone remembers this approval for all future course documents.
6
Save PDF to Files
The lecture PDF view opens immediately. Tap the Share button ([↑]) > Save to Files, choose your folder (iCloud Drive or On My iPhone), and tap Save. Your lecture is now saved for offline study!

Bookmark Any Page in Safari
In Safari, tap the Share button ([↑]) > Add Bookmark. Save it to Favorites.
💡
Custom Bookmark Name: You don't have to name it "pdf" — feel free to name it Download Lecture or Parul PDF!

Paste the Code into the Bookmark URL
