Step-by-Step Installation Walkthrough

Choose your browser below to view the simple 1-minute setup steps:

1
Download & Extract the ZIP File
Click the blue Download Extension (.zip) button above. When downloaded, right-click the file, choose Extract All..., and extract the folder to a permanent location (e.g. your Documents folder).
2
Open the Extensions Management Page
In Google Chrome or Brave, open a new tab, type chrome://extensions into the address bar, and press Enter. (If you use Microsoft Edge, navigate to edge://extensions).
3
Enable Developer Mode
Look at the top-right corner of the Extensions page. Toggle the Developer mode switch to ON. A new action bar will appear with three buttons.
4
Click "Load Unpacked" & Select the Folder
Click the Load unpacked button on the top-left toolbar. In the file explorer, navigate into your extracted folder and click Select Folder.
💡
Pin the Extension: Click the puzzle icon (🧩) next to your browser's address bar and click the pin icon next to Parul eLearning PDF Download so it stays visible!
5
Open Any Lecture & Download!
Navigate to your course portal on elearning.paruluniversity.ac.in. Whenever a lecture document or presentation is open, the floating blue Download PDF button appears automatically in the bottom-right corner!
1
Download & Extract the Firefox ZIP
Click the orange Download Firefox (.zip) button above. Right-click the downloaded archive and extract it on your computer.
2
Open Firefox Debugging
In Mozilla Firefox, type about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox into the address bar and press Enter.
3
Load Temporary Add-on
Click the Load Temporary Add-on... button. In the file picker, select the manifest.json file located inside your extracted folder.
4
Start Studying Offline!
Open your course page on Parul University eLearning. The floating HUD triggers automatically whenever a course document is detected!
1
Download & Extract the Safari ZIP
Click the blue Download Safari (.zip) button above. Double-click the downloaded archive to extract it on your Mac.
2
Enable Safari Developer Menu
Open Apple Safari, click Safari in the top menu bar > Settings (or Preferences) > Advanced tab. Check the box for "Show features for web developers" (or "Show Develop menu").
3
Allow Unsigned Extensions
In Safari's top menu bar, click the newly visible Develop menu, and ensure "Allow Unsigned Extensions" is enabled.
4
Activate in Safari Extensions
In Safari Settings > Extensions, check the box next to Parul eLearning PDF Download. Open any course lecture on elearning.paruluniversity.ac.in and the download button triggers automatically!
